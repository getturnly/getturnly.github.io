What Turnly is

Turnly is coordination software for short-term-rental turnover cleaning. Hosts post and pay for cleanings; cleaners claim, perform, and get paid for them. Cleaners are independent providers — Turnly is not a party to the cleaning services themselves and does not employ cleaners.

Subscriptions

Hosting plans (Free, Starter, Growth, Pro, Unlimited) and the Calendar Sync add-on are auto-renewable monthly subscriptions billed through your Apple ID. They renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and can be managed or cancelled in your App Store account settings. Plan limits apply per team.

Payments & fees

  • Host-to-cleaner payments are processed by Stripe from the host's saved card when the host approves a completed cleaning.
  • A 4.5% + $0.25 payment fee is added to host payments.
  • Cleaner instant cash-outs carry a 2.5% fee; standard transfers are free.
  • Cleaner payouts require completing Stripe identity verification. Approved payments are final in-app; refunds for service disputes are between host and cleaner.
