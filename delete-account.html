Delete Your Turnly Account

Turnly - Turnover Cleaning (com.getturnly.app). Last updated October 19, 2026.

You can delete your Turnly account and your personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open the Turnly app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Team or Profile tab.
  3. Tap "Delete my account" and confirm.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you cannot sign in, email lenoandy@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will verify the request came from the account's email address and complete the deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (name and email address)
  • Your profile information
  • Your personal data that is not needed for a teammate's completed job records

What is retained

Completed job records that teammates rely on (for example, records of cleanings that were finished and paid) are kept with your identity removed, so the other members of your team keep an accurate history. Payment processing records held by our payment provider, Stripe, are retained by Stripe under its own policies.

Timing

In-app deletion takes effect when you confirm it. Email requests are completed promptly after verification.
